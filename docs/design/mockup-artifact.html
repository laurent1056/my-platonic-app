Entry No. 001

Frying Pan

Declared
PP-001
Plate 001 · specimen
Lodge 12″ Cast Iron Skilletmodel L10SK3 · cast iron · rebuildable
$30–40
Form definition
12-inch cast iron with 3–4 mm walls holds thermal mass and resists temperature drops.
Why it ends the search
Inert material — no coatings to fail. Seasoning improves with use as polymerized oil accumulates; it gets better with time. Rust and cracks are repairable. Made since 1896 to the same specs.
Permanence mechanism
Repairable
Key disqualifiers
  • Non-stick (Teflon/ceramic) coatings degrade in 2–5 years — mandatory replacement.
  • Stainless never improves; hot spots permanent unless $300+ multi-ply.
  • Premium cast iron (Le Creuset, Finex) — identical iron at 5× the price.
Admission test
Sufficient thermal mass
No disposable coating
Repair path via re-seasoning

Why the search stays open
Every refrigerator post-2000 contains irrecoverable failure points: sealed compressor systems with proprietary refrigerants ($600–900 plus labor — 50–80% of a new unit); electronic control boards that fail unpredictably; and a repair threshold that crosses replacement cost. Even Sub-Zero ($8K–15K) uses identical sealed systems.
What the category would require
Mechanical thermostats, not electronic boards
Compressor as a replaceable module (<40% of new cost)
A 20-year parts guarantee
Published repair manuals
Standing verdict
Empty is not a gap in the register — it is a finding. The entry stays open, and public, until a product clears the admission test.
