# Constitutional Batch 09 — Categories 84–93

**Research date:** 2026-09-10  
**Constitution:** 1.1.0  
**Backlog span:** Electronics 84–90, Household Systems 91–93

## What this batch is

This is the next constitutional research pass through the original Top 100
backlog. The inherited CSV is historical register material; it is not treated
as a terminal ruling ledger. Each category is defined before a product is
considered, and a broad label is split when it hides materially different
Forms.

The batch continues the declaration-seeking interpretation of Constitution
1.1.0:

- `EMPTY` remains a last resort after a real rescue pass.
- Ordinary maintenance, replaceable wear parts, and rational renewal are not
  automatic failures.
- A category may be declared only inside a Form that is coherent, useful, and
  honestly scoped.
- A parent category may remain `SPLIT_REQUIRED` while one or more child Forms
  move toward declaration.
- These are research dispositions, not terminal public rulings. A terminal
  ruling still requires the complete evidence packet, five passing gates,
  hard-disqualifier review, counter-case, confidence floor, adjudicator, and
  public receipt.

## The ten determinations

### 84 — Television

**Parent disposition:** Preserve `SPLIT_REQUIRED`. “Television” currently
contains at least four materially different Forms:

1. a flat-panel living-room television with an integrated smart platform;
2. a flat-panel display driven by an external source;
3. a commercial/signage display with a different service and duty cycle; and
4. a legacy or analog display whose permanence and repair logic is different
   from a current OLED/LCD panel.

**Why the parent cannot receive one declaration:** a flat-panel TV's panel is
often the critical functional core, and independent repair guidance notes that
panel failure is usually not economically repairable except under warranty.
The smart layer also creates a separate software-dependency question. Board,
port, power-supply, and backlight repairs do not resolve a failed panel or an
unsupported platform. That is a material Form and permanence conflict, not a
minor brand preference.

**Declaration-rescue path:** create a child case for an ordinary
external-source display only if the category is intentionally about a display
as a durable image surface rather than a cloud-dependent television. Create a
separate child case for current home-cinema TVs only if panel replacement,
software longevity, and repair economics can be proven. No exact product is
named yet because naming one would currently hide the central failure mode.

**Counter-case / open proof:** a commercial display, a projector, a used
plasma, and a simple monitor may each be defensible in a different Form. They
must not be silently substituted for a universal household television.

**Sources:** [iFixit television repair guidance](https://www.ifixit.com/Device/Television),
[iFixit panel-failure guidance](https://www.ifixit.com/Troubleshooting/Television/Lines%2BOn%2BTV%2BScreen/505119).

### 85 — Router

**Form:** A wired home or small-office router/firewall appliance for an owner
who is willing to use a separate wireless access point when needed. The Form
requires standard network interfaces, an operating system that is not tied to
one vendor cloud, replaceable storage and memory where practical, documented
firmware paths, and a chassis that can remain in service as software changes.
Exclude all-in-one consumer mesh systems, enterprise chassis, and Wi-Fi
access points judged as though they were wired routers.

**Proposed declaration:** Protectli **Vault Pro VP2430e**, exact SKU `VP2430e`.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the current VP2430e is a fanless,
aluminum, four-port 2.5GbE appliance with a replaceable DDR5 SO-DIMM, NVMe
slot, optional wireless/modem slots, coreboot support, and OS-agnostic use as
a firewall/router. The product is not being declared as “the best Wi-Fi
router”; it is being considered as a stable wired network core. The absence of
moving parts and the ability to replace the storage and memory materially
change the failure path compared with sealed consumer gateways.

**Counter-case / open proof:** a MikroTik hAP ax3 or another integrated
wireless router may be the better Form for one-box household Wi-Fi. That is a
different object with a different radio, firmware, and service boundary. The
case must still verify Protectli's long-term firmware support, exact memory and
storage compatibility, independent reliability evidence, and the practical
cost of replacing the board or power supply.

**Sources:** [Protectli VP2430e product page](https://protectli.com/product/vp2430e/),
[Protectli warranty](https://protectli.com/warranty-and-returns/),
[Protectli knowledge base](https://kb.protectli.com/),
[MikroTik hAP ax3](https://mikrotik.com/product/hap_ax3).

### 86 — Headphones

**Form:** Wired, open-back, over-ear reference headphones for home listening
and critical evaluation. Exclude wireless headphones, active noise-cancelling
headphones, earbuds, and closed-back isolation headphones; their batteries,
electronics, acoustic goals, and failure modes create different Forms.

**Proposed declaration:** Sennheiser **HD 600**, current service record
`508824`.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the critical signal path is simple and
the highest-wear interfaces are explicitly replaceable. Sennheiser publishes
replacement cables, earpads, headband padding, and a service path for the HD
600. Its manual shows cable and earpad replacement, and the current spare-parts
catalog identifies the model separately rather than hiding it inside a vague
family label. This is a strong example of a product whose ordinary wear does
not require discarding the useful core.

**Counter-case / open proof:** the HD 650, HD 6XX, and newer HD 620S may win
for a different tonal, impedance, or closed-back Form. Those differences do
not automatically overturn the HD 600 for this Form, but the terminal packet
needs one independent listening/longevity source and a current price and parts
check. The ruling must say that it is not a universal verdict on wireless or
portable listening.

**Sources:** [Sennheiser HD 600 service and spares](https://spares.sennheiser-hearing.com/catalog/product/508824-hd-600),
[HD 600 replacement earpads](https://us.sennheiser-hearing.com/products/annular-earpads-with-disk-pair),
[HD 600 manual](https://cdn.sennheiser-hearing.com/product-documents/product-downloads/hd-600/instruction_manual/Instruction_manual_HD_600.pdf).

### 87 — Speakers

**Form:** A passive, high-sensitivity living-room loudspeaker for long-horizon
stereo listening, with no built-in amplifier, battery, network account, or
cloud service. Exclude powered speakers, soundbars, subwoofers, nearfield
studio monitors, and portable Bluetooth speakers.

**Proposed declaration:** Klipsch **Heresy IV**.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the Form removes the most terminating
electronic dependencies before evaluation. The Heresy IV has a cabinet,
replaceable external signal path, conventional binding posts, passive
cross-over network, and a long-running product lineage. Klipsch identifies the
current Heresy IV as a three-way, high-sensitivity passive speaker, assembled
in the United States, and provides a ten-year Heritage warranty that includes
repair or replacement for covered defects. The model is expensive and room-
dependent, but those are disclosed Form constraints rather than evidence that
the category cannot have an ideal.

**Counter-case / open proof:** Harbeth, JBL, KEF, and other passive designs
may be better for different room sizes, dispersion preferences, or nearfield
use. Before terminal issue, the case must establish why the selected Form is
about a durable high-sensitivity living-room instrument rather than “the best
speaker for every listener,” and it must verify long-term driver, crossover,
grille, and service-part availability.

**Sources:** [Klipsch Heresy IV](https://www.klipsch.com/products/heresy-iv-floorstanding-speaker),
[Heresy IV specification sheet](https://assets.klipsch.com/product-specsheets/Heresy-IV-Spec-Sheet-v04.pdf),
[Klipsch Heritage warranty](https://www.klipsch.com/warranty-heritage-products/).

### 88 — Camera

**Parent disposition:** Preserve `SPLIT_REQUIRED`. “Camera” combines film and
digital systems, fixed and interchangeable lenses, still and video work,
compact and professional bodies, and radically different repair economies.
The product that is ideal for an everyday fixed-lens film practice is not the
product that is ideal for interchangeable-lens digital work or video.

**Declaration-rescue paths:** at least three child cases are warranted:

1. a new compact film camera for ordinary 35 mm carry;
2. a repairable mechanical 35 mm SLR system, likely adjudicated through the
   used market and service ecosystem; and
3. a digital interchangeable-lens camera with a stable mount, service path,
   and long-lived lens ecosystem.

The current PENTAX 17 is a useful candidate for the first child Form: it is a
new half-frame 35 mm camera with a fixed 25 mm lens, manual film advance,
zone-focus controls, and a published repair/warranty path. It is not yet a
universal camera ideal, and its electronic shutter, built-in flash, and
manufacturer-specific parts require a complete case before declaration.

**Counter-case / open proof:** Nikon FM2/Nikon F, Pentax K-series, Leica M,
Fujifilm X-series, and current Sony/Canon bodies all become relevant only after
the child Form is named. The parent should not name a winner while the object
being judged can change from a film camera to a video platform without notice.

**Sources:** [PENTAX 17 product page](https://us.ricoh-imaging.com/product/pentax-17/),
[Ricoh support and repairs](https://us.ricoh-imaging.com/support/),
[PENTAX 17 extended warranty](https://us.ricoh-imaging.com/warranty-information-pentax17/).

### 89 — Smartwatch

**Parent disposition:** Keep the parent `CONDITIONAL` until the child Form is
made explicit. A smartwatch can be a phone companion, health monitor, outdoor
GPS instrument, safety device, or fashion object. These uses do not share one
practical ceiling or one acceptable failure path.

**Child Form with a declaration path:** A rugged outdoor GPS watch for
navigation, timekeeping, activity recording, and basic phone notifications,
where long battery life and physical resilience matter more than a full app
ecosystem. Exclude medical diagnosis, phone replacement, and fashion/luxury
smartwatch Forms.

**Proposed subject:** Garmin **Instinct 3 Solar, 50 mm**, working identifier
`010-02935-02`.

**Why the path is promising:** Garmin documents a 10 ATM rugged watch with a
reinforced bezel, solar charging, and up to 40 days of ordinary smartwatch
use or unlimited use under its stated solar conditions. The low-power display
and field-oriented design fit the child Form better than a sealed glass phone
companion. Band replacement is straightforward and ordinary use does not
require a daily cloud service.

**Material condition:** the rechargeable lithium-ion battery is built in and
not user-replaceable. That is not hidden in the case. The candidate can only
become `DECLARED` if Garmin's authorized service/replacement path, software
support horizon, and replacement economics establish rational renewal of the
critical battery assembly. If that evidence fails, the correct child status is
`CONSUMABLE`, not a universal `EMPTY` ruling for all watches.

**Sources:** [Garmin Instinct 3 Solar specifications and battery life](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-2C274FD2-F0C3-445C-B0AC-700FECCE12E9/EN-US/GUID-64755EF0-CC50-4E94-B0B8-17D6EBD62A23.html),
[Instinct 3 Solar manual](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-2C274FD2-F0C3-445C-B0AC-700FECCE12E9/EN-US/Instinct_3_Solar_OM_EN-US.pdf),
[Garmin battery guidance](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=LLgTVPWsPH7tc5v4lwEST9&identifier=010-02293-00&tab=topics),
[iFixit smartwatch repairability framework](https://www.ifixit.com/repairability/smartwatch-repairability-scores).

### 90 — Home Thermostat

**Form:** A non-cloud-dependent, programmable low-voltage thermostat for a
conventional or heat-pump residential HVAC system, with a standard wiring and
mounting interface, local controls, published installation documentation, and
a rational module-replacement path. Exclude line-voltage heaters, hydronic
floor controls, industrial controls, and smart thermostats whose essential
operation depends on a vendor account or cloud service.

**Proposed declaration:** Honeywell Home **T6 Pro Programmable**, SKU
`TH6220U2000/U`.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the T6 Pro is a defined low-voltage,
programmable module with a published installation/user-documentation set and
the UWP mounting system shared across the T Series. It uses local scheduling
and does not require Wi-Fi for the Form. A thermostat is not a cast-iron pan:
its rational permanence mechanism is standardized replacement of a small
control module, not rebuilding every relay on a kitchen table. Batteries,
wall plates, sensors, documentation, and professional installation support
are part of the case.

**Counter-case / open proof:** a mechanical thermostat may have a simpler
failure path, while a smart thermostat may offer materially better energy
management for a different Form. The final packet needs exact compatibility
boundaries, current parts/wall-plate availability, and one independent
assessment of service life. The declaration must never imply that the T6 Pro is
safe to install without verifying the HVAC system and local electrical rules.

**Sources:** [Resideo T6 Pro Programmable](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/products/air/thermostats/programmable-thermostats/t6-pro-programmable-thermostat-up-to-2-heat-2-cool-th6220u2000-u/),
[T6 Pro installation documentation](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/products/air/thermostats/wifi-thermostats/t6-pro-smart-thermostat-multi-stage-3-heat-2-cool-th6320wf2003-u/).

### 91 — Washing Machine

**Form:** A full-size residential top-load washer with a conventional agitator
and local controls, judged for ordinary household laundry, durable mechanical
assemblies, service access, and a rational parts path. Exclude front-load
washers, compact units, laundromat/commercial machines, and high-efficiency
impeller machines whose Form changes the wash action and trade-offs.

**Proposed declaration:** Speed Queen **TC5**, white, product code
`TC5003WN`.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the TC5 has a stable exact identifier,
local controls, a conventional wash action, and a five-year parts-and-labor
warranty on the current product page. Speed Queen maintains model-specific
manuals, service requests, authorized service locations, and a parts channel.
This is a heavy appliance with a service ecosystem, not an attempt to pretend
that every household washer has the same energy, capacity, or loading Form.

**Counter-case / open proof:** a front-load washer may materially outperform
the TC5 on water and energy use, and a simpler older mechanical model may have
a different permanence story. Those are serious counter-cases. The terminal
packet needs an independent reliability/repair record, current warranty
terms, service-part prices, and a clear statement that the Form prioritizes
traditional top-load serviceability over maximum efficiency or capacity.

**Sources:** [Speed Queen TC5003WN](https://speedqueen.com/products/top-load-washers/tc5003wn/),
[Speed Queen support and parts](https://speedqueen.com/support/),
[Speed Queen manuals and downloads](https://speedqueen.com/support/manuals-downloads/).

### 92 — Dryer

**Form:** A full-size vented electric residential dryer with local controls,
serviceable heating and drum systems, and a stable parts/service path. Exclude
gas dryers, heat-pump dryers, compact units, and commercial laundry systems;
fuel source and thermal architecture materially change the Form.

**Proposed declaration:** Speed Queen **DC5**, white, product code
`DC5004WE`.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the current DC5 has a stable product code,
galvanized cylinder, reversible door, electric heating system, moisture sensor,
published installation/user documentation, a five-year parts-and-labor
warranty, and a manufacturer-supported service and parts network. The dryer
still has electronics and convenience features; the case does not claim that
they are immortal. The declaration path rests on the appliance's repairable
mechanical and thermal assemblies plus rational module replacement.

**Counter-case / open proof:** the DR3 or a simpler mechanical dryer may have
a stronger simplicity claim, while a heat-pump dryer may win the energy Form.
The final case must compare service manuals, critical parts, repair costs,
venting requirements, and model drift before declaring the DC5.

**Sources:** [Speed Queen DC5004WE](https://speedqueen.com/products/electric-dryers/dc5004we/),
[Speed Queen service/support](https://speedqueen.com/support/),
[DC5 current specification sheet](https://speedqueen.com/wp-content/themes/speedqueen-24-v2-5/assets-dryer/materials/AR25-0009_SpecSheet_Evolution-DR3-DC5_en-US.pdf).

### 93 — Water Heater

**Parent disposition:** Preserve `SPLIT_REQUIRED`. Gas storage, electric
storage, heat-pump hybrid, tankless, point-of-use, and commercial systems have
different safety, installation, service, and replacement Forms. A 50-gallon
electric storage heater cannot be declared as the universal ideal of all water
heaters.

**Child Form:** A 50-gallon, high-efficiency electric storage water heater for
an ordinary residential replacement where standard tank dimensions, service
access, anode protection, replaceable heating elements, and a documented
professional service path matter. Exclude gas venting, tankless demand,
heat-pump-only efficiency comparisons, and commercial recovery rates.

**Proposed child declaration:** Rheem **Performance Platinum Gladiator**,
working model `XE50M12CG55U0`.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the current Gladiator platform lists
replaceable copper heating elements, a premium-grade anode rod, diagnostic
mode, a durable brass drain valve, leak detection/prevention, and a twelve-
year limited tank-and-parts warranty with three years of in-home labor. A
storage tank is an inherently renewing core: the tank eventually reaches the
end of its safe service life, but elements, anode, controls, diagnostics, and
valves can extend the useful interval. The case must treat professional
installation and pressure/temperature safety as part of the Form.

**Counter-case / open proof:** a heat-pump water heater may be the better
efficiency Form, and Bradford White may provide a stronger professional-only
service ecosystem. The terminal case must confirm the exact capacity/model,
current warranty certificate, anode and element availability, service cost,
local code constraints, and the replacement threshold for the tank itself.

**Sources:** [Rheem Performance Platinum Gladiator](https://www.rheem.com/product/rheem-performance-platinum-gladiator-with-demand-response-xe40m12cg55u0/),
[Rheem heat-pump water-heater range](https://www.rheem.com/heatpumpwaterheater/),
[Bradford White warranty center](https://warrantycenter.bradfordwhite.com/).

## What should happen next

1. Create the child Forms before creating terminal cases for Television,
   Camera, Smartwatch, and Water Heater. The site should eventually show the
   parent as unresolved while linking to any declared child Forms.
2. Create candidate case files for VP2430e, HD 600, Heresy IV, T6 Pro,
   TC5003WN, DC5004WE, and the selected Rheem model. Keep them nonterminal
   until the evidence ledger contains the required independent material.
3. Create one conditional child case for the Garmin Instinct 3 Solar and make
   the battery/service question a first-class gate rather than burying it in a
   note.
4. Add at least one independent source to every proposed declaration path;
   manufacturer pages establish specifications and service claims but do not
   independently prove longevity or comparative superiority.
5. Record exact model identifiers, variants, jurisdiction, installation
   boundaries, and re-review triggers before migrating any result into the
   public register.
6. After categories 94–100 are researched, consolidate the completed register
   around `Category → Form → Case → Ruling → Product → Evidence`. That
   normalized structure is the foundation for mature category navigation,
   filters, product dossiers, and transparent parent/child pages.

The batch is intentionally more willing to find declaration paths than the old
Gemini-era register, but it does not erase category boundaries to improve the
headline percentage. That is how the register can become authoritative: the
reader can see exactly what was declared, what was scoped, and what remains
unresolved.
