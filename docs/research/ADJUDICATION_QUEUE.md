# Adjudication queue

The 100-record register is now backed by formal case files for every
nonterminal row. These files are deliberately nonterminal: they capture the
Form, proposed subject, failure modes, counter-case, and evidence gaps without
pretending that a research disposition is a constitutional ruling.

## Current queue shape

- 40 nonterminal register records have case files.
- `SPLIT_REQUIRED` records are category-boundary cases, not failed products.
- `CONDITIONAL` records name the operating condition that still needs proof.
- `CANDIDATE` records have a plausible declaration path but lack a complete
  evidence packet and adjudication.
- `CONSUMABLE` records are judged by rational renewal rather than false
  permanence.

## Promotion rule

A case may move to `DECLARED` or `EMPTY` only when its five gates, hard
disqualifiers, evidence floor, counter-case, confidence floor, and human
adjudication are complete. The public CSV remains a migration-compatible view;
the case file is the authority for promotion.

## Recommended first adjudication wave

Begin with the strongest declaration paths that have exact subjects and
serviceable failure modes: the three-season sleeping bag, hard cooler,
liquid-fuel backpacking stove, repairable laptop, wired headphones, home
thermostat, top-load washer, vented electric dryer, corded bagged vacuum, and
closed-comb safety razor. Keep broad parent cases—pocket knife, smartphone,
desktop computer, television, camera, water heater, furnace, and air
conditioner—split until their child Forms are represented explicitly.
