# Legacy child rows migrated from the founding register

The first 68-row CSV contained four provisional child rows in positions
65–68. The completed constitutional research uses those positions for the
next sequence of category records, so the four rows were moved out of the
canonical register during the 100-record migration rather than silently
discarded.

Their useful Form work is preserved here:

| Former row | Former child | Preserved editorial constraint |
|---:|---|---|
| 65 | Task Chair (New) | Require one exact model with stable parts support for casters, cylinder, arms, seat, and upholstery before declaration. |
| 66 | Task Chair (Used) | A used-market chair may be viable when frame, mechanism, sourcing rules, acceptable wear, and refurbishment economics are explicit. |
| 67 | Food Storage Container (Glass + Lid) | The glass body should remain usable while lid and gasket replacement is proven across the model lineage. |
| 68 | Food Storage Container (Dry Goods) | Pantry storage is a different Form from leftover storage; airtight seals, latches, and replacement availability are the critical questions. |

These rows remain available in git history and in this migration record. Future
parent/Form data should represent them as child Forms beneath `Task Chair` and
`Food Storage Container`, rather than restoring them as duplicate top-level
categories.
