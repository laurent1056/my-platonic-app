## The determinations

### 65 — Bed Frame

The coherent Form is not “any bed furniture.” It is a support structure that
holds a mattress at a useful height for decades with few failure points. The
research favors a simple heavy-duty steel frame with L-shaped brackets, a
center support, adjustable sizing, and bolt-together construction. Its ordinary
maintenance is tightening hardware; bent brackets or supports are mechanical
repair problems rather than reasons to discard the entire frame.

The subject is not yet an exact product identity. A terminal case must decide
whether a commodity model family can be named without pretending that every
similar listing has the same steel gauge, weight rating, or fitment.

**Counter-case:** wood frames can be repaired and may be better furniture, but
humidity-sensitive joints, decorative complexity, and variable construction
make the simple steel Form more stable for the narrow function.

### 66 — Sofa / Couch

This is a difficult category because the Form includes both a structural frame
and human-comfort materials that predictably wear. A candidate must have a
kiln-dried hardwood frame, durable spring construction, replaceable or
reupholsterable cushions and covers, and an ownership horizon long enough to
make refurbishment rational.

The prior research treated a used IKEA Karlstad with replaceable covers and a
high-quality new sofa such as the Crate & Barrel Lounge II as conditional
paths, not co-declarations. The unresolved question is whether the cost of
reupholstery and foam replacement remains rational for ordinary users. This
should stay `CONDITIONAL` until the case chooses one bounded Form and one
defensible subject—or explicitly issues `EMPTY` for the mass-market parent.

### 67 — Bookshelf

The Form is a load-bearing shelf system whose geometry prevents sag under the
weight of books. The key specification is not prestige wood; it is shelf span,
thickness, joinery, and resistance to racking. The research identifies two
credible construction paths: a solid-wood or hardwood-plywood build with
bounded spans, and a bolt-together industrial steel shelf with adjustable
levels and a documented load rating.

The terminal case must not publish both as simultaneous ideals. It should
choose whether the Form is a build specification that can be fulfilled by
multiple suppliers or an exact industrial product. Flat-pack particleboard
systems are disqualified by sag, chipped surfaces, and loosening cam-locks.

### 68 — Coffee Table

The working Form is a low living-room surface with a refinishable solid-wood
top and simple legs or trestle base. Drawers, storage mechanisms, glass, stone,
and veneer-over-particleboard add failure modes without improving the essential
function. A used vintage table is admissible when its construction is proven
and the surface can be repaired or refinished.

The case needs to resolve whether “solid wood, simple construction” is a
permitted construction Form or whether the register must name one exact model.
The prior research favors the construction Form because furniture supply is
locally variable and the ruling depends more on material and geometry than on
one brand badge.

### 69 — Dresser / Wardrobe

The broad parent combines two related but different storage functions. The
research supports a conditional solid-wood dresser Form: proven hardwood,
dovetail or robust drawer joinery, replaceable pulls, serviceable slides, and a
refinishable surface. Vintage and used examples are attractive because their
survival is already evidence of durability.

Modern mass-market particleboard dressers and cheap wardrobes remain a
separate compromised field. A terminal case should either split dresser from
wardrobe or narrow the category explicitly. It should not elevate a generic
“solid wood dresser” while leaving construction, hardware, and sourcing rules
undefined.

### 70 — Desk Lamp

The working Form is an architect-style task lamp with a spring-balanced arm,
metal structure, a weighted or clamp base, a standard E26 socket, and a simple
switch. Standard bulbs keep the lamp independent of any one LED technology;
socket, cord, switch, and spring are the relevant service interfaces.

Integrated LED lamps, touch controls, fragile plastic friction hinges, and
sealed decorative electronics fail the Form because the light source or control
system makes the whole lamp obsolete. The exact subject still needs a model
identity and parts/service receipt before declaration.

### 71 — Floor Lamp

The working Form is a simple metal torchiere or arc lamp: stable weighted base,
standard E26 socket, minimal wiring, and a simple rotary or pull-chain switch.
The ordinary failure path is a bulb, cord, socket, or switch that can be
replaced. Multi-head tree lamps and integrated LED fixtures introduce
unnecessary electrical and mechanical complexity.

The case must select one geometry. A torchiere and an adjustable arc lamp solve
different lighting needs; they should not be presented as two ideals under one
unbounded parent.

### 72 — Window Blinds

The research found no honest universal declaration. Horizontal and vertical
blinds, cellular shades, roller shades, and Roman shades all contain different
but predictable failure modes: cords fray, springs fatigue, slats bend,
tracks jam, fabrics tear, and plastic controls strip. Premium pricing does not
remove the underlying replacement cycle.

The result is an `EMPTY` research path for the broad blinds category. The
apophatic requirement is clear: a viable Form would need user-replaceable
lifting parts, a non-fatiguing mechanism, durable slats or fabric, and a
long-horizon parts guarantee. Curtains or shutters may be better subjects, but
they are different categories and should not be smuggled in as a substitute.

### 73 — Pillow

The research treats the pillow as intrinsically consumable. Down, latex,
memory foam, synthetic fill, buckwheat, and kapok each lose loft, resilience,
or structural integrity on a predictable cycle. Fluffing or refilling can
extend some products but does not turn the category into a durable object with
a stable Form.

The honest result is an `EMPTY` research path for a durable pillow ideal. A
future rational-consumption case could compare replacement economics and
comfort, but that would be a different editorial product from declaring a
permanent ideal.
