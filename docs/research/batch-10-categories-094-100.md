# Constitutional Batch 10 — Categories 94–100

**Research date:** 2026-09-10  
**Constitution:** 1.1.0  
**Backlog span:** Household Systems 94–96, Personal Care & Misc 97–100

## What this batch is

This pass completes the original Top 100 category backlog. Completion here
means that every category has been defined, rescued where possible, and given
a research disposition. It does not mean that every candidate is already a
terminal public ruling. Terminal declarations still require the constitutional
case packet, evidence receipt, independent evidence, counter-case, confidence
floor, adjudication, and public ruling event.

The declaration-seeking standard remains active:

- Narrow the Form when the parent category hides incompatible systems.
- Treat ordinary service, maintenance, wear parts, and rational renewal as
  compatible with permanence when they are practical.
- Treat an inherently renewing object as `CONSUMABLE` when that is the honest
  permanence mechanism.
- Do not use a product's preferred features to reverse-engineer a category
  after evaluation begins.
- Do not make the whole category `EMPTY` merely because its first broad
  definition was impossible.

## The seven determinations

### 94 — Furnace

**Parent disposition:** Preserve `SPLIT_REQUIRED`. “Furnace” can mean gas,
propane, electric resistance, oil, heat-pump backup, packaged equipment, or a
commercial unit. Even within gas forced-air systems, capacity, orientation,
venting, climate, and code requirements determine the actual Form. One exact
model cannot be declared for every house without turning a sizing constraint
into a hidden preference.

**Child Form with a declaration path:** A residential, high-efficiency,
two-stage gas forced-air furnace for a defined capacity and upflow/downflow
installation, with a professional service ecosystem, available controls,
replaceable blower/ignition/safety components, and a documented heat-exchanger
support term. The load calculation and jurisdiction are part of the category;
they are not optional buyer personalization.

**Working subject:** Trane **Premier 97 Two-Stage Variable Speed Gas Furnace**,
the current S9V2 platform. The current product page lists exact model variants
such as `S9V2C100U5VSB`; the final case must choose one capacity and orientation
after the Form is defined rather than declaring the S9V2 family as a whole.

**Why the path is promising:** Trane publishes model specifications, dealer
service, and registered warranty terms that include a long heat-exchanger
period and a parts period. The furnace is a serviceable professional system,
not a sealed countertop appliance. The case must still establish critical
ignition, control-board, inducer, blower, and heat-exchanger service paths and
the point at which a repair is no longer rational.

**Counter-case / open proof:** Carrier Performance 96 is a serious counter-
case, as are electric and heat-pump systems. They may win different Forms or
different climates. The case must not use efficiency alone to declare a winner,
and it must state that gas combustion, venting, installation, and local code
are safety boundaries requiring a qualified professional.

**Sources:** [Trane Premier 97 / S9V2](https://www.trane.com/residential/en/products/furnaces/premier-97/),
[Trane warranty terms](https://www.trane.com/pdf/GW-658-2032.pdf),
[Carrier Performance 96](https://www.carrier.com/us/en/residential/furnaces/59sp6/).

### 95 — Air Conditioner

**Parent disposition:** Preserve `SPLIT_REQUIRED`. Central split air
conditioners, ductless mini-splits, window units, portable units, packaged
systems, and heat pumps do not share one Form. Refrigerant, compressor,
installation, controls, service access, and capacity all change the admission
test.

**Declaration-rescue paths:** create at least two child cases:

1. a residential central split air conditioner for a defined tonnage,
   refrigerant, climate, and duct system; and
2. a ductless mini-split/heat-pump system for a defined room or zone.

Both child Forms have plausible declaration paths because professional HVAC
systems are modular and have manufacturer/dealer service networks. But the
exact model depends on load, electrical service, refrigerant rules, indoor
coil, and installation geometry. Choosing a famous flagship as the universal
air-conditioner ideal would fail model stability and category coherence.

**Working research direction:** Trane's XV-series central system and
Mitsubishi Electric's M-Series ductless system are useful counter-case anchors,
not terminal winners. The next cases should start with one explicit capacity
and architecture, then record the exact indoor/outdoor matched system.

**Counter-case / open proof:** a window unit may be the more repairable and
economical Form for a renter; a heat pump may be the stronger climate and
energy Form; a portable unit may be the only legal option in a particular
building. Those are not runner-up products. They are evidence that the parent
must split before a declaration can be honest.

**Sources:** [Trane air conditioners](https://www.trane.com/residential/en/products/air-conditioners/choice-15-wg/),
[Trane XV20i context](https://www.trane.com/residential/en/resources/blog/meet-xv20i-tranes-top-performing-air-conditioner/),
[Mitsubishi Electric wall-mounted systems](https://www.mitsubishicomfort.com/products/wall-mounted),
[Mitsubishi warranty information](https://www.mitsubishicomfort.com/warranties).

### 96 — Vacuum Cleaner

**Form:** A corded, bagged upright vacuum for ordinary residential mixed-floor
cleaning, with an owner-usable path for replacing bags, filters, belts,
brushes, hoses, switches, and other expected wear or service parts. Exclude
cordless stick vacuums, robots, wet/dry shop vacuums, central vacuums, and
canisters; they create materially different portability and failure Forms.

**Proposed declaration:** SEBO **FELIX Premium**, current working variant
`Rosso #9809AM`.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** SEBO publishes an exact active model,
exploded parts schematics, service documentation, genuine spare parts, and a
repair/warranty channel. The FELIX has a corded suction motor, replaceable
brush roller, removable power head, tool-free brush removal, replaceable bags
and filters, thermal protection, and a long cord. SEBO's US warranty covers
motors for seven years and non-wear parts and labor for five years, with a
dealer/service network. The model's core can remain useful while its bags,
filters, belt, brush, and external parts are renewed.

**Counter-case / open proof:** a SEBO E3 canister or a simpler mechanical
upright may win a different Form. Dyson and other cordless systems may win for
convenience, but their battery path changes the permanence test. Before
terminal issue, obtain independent mixed-floor performance and long-term
service evidence, and record the US jurisdiction explicitly.

**Sources:** [SEBO FELIX Premium](https://sebo.us/product/felix),
[SEBO FELIX parts and schematic](https://cdn.sebo.us/corporate/upright/felix-dart/documents/FELIX-Manual.pdf),
[SEBO warranty and support](https://sebo.us/warranty-and-support),
[SEBO spare parts](https://sebo.us/spare-parts/9809AM/SP).

### 97 — Toothbrush

**Form:** A manual, soft-bristle toothbrush for ordinary adult daily brushing,
where the bristle field is intentionally renewed every few months. The Form
does not claim immortality for a hygiene tool whose functional surface is
supposed to wear and be replaced.

**Proposed subject:** Curaprox **CS 5460** manual soft-bristle toothbrush.

**Recommended status:** `CONSUMABLE`, not `EMPTY` and not a false permanent
`DECLARED`. The American Dental Association recommends soft bristles and
replacement approximately every three to four months or sooner when the
bristles are matted or frayed. Curaprox's current CS 5460 material identifies
the thin, dense filaments and an ergonomic handle; those facts make it a
credible candidate for the defined hygiene Form. The replacement cycle is the
product's rational permanence mechanism.

**Why this matters:** a consumable ruling is not a failure of the philosophy.
It is the honest answer when renewing the functional surface is safer and more
rational than preserving the object indefinitely. The public page should make
the renewal cadence explicit and avoid implying clinical advice beyond the
evidence.

**Counter-case / open proof:** powered toothbrushes, orthodontic brushes,
children's brushes, and specialized periodontal tools require separate Forms.
The final record needs an exact CS 5460 variant, an independent dental
efficacy/ergonomics source, and a clear statement that individual dental needs
belong with a dental professional.

**Sources:** [American Dental Association toothbrush guidance](https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/toothbrushes),
[Curaprox CS 5460 material](https://enjoy.curaprox.com/en-gb/duoit).

### 98 — Razor

**Form:** A reusable closed-comb, double-edge safety razor for ordinary facial
shaving, using replaceable standardized blades. Exclude cartridge systems,
straight razors, electric shavers, body-specific razors, and professional
barber tools.

**Proposed declaration:** MERKUR **34C**, SKU `34001`.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the handle and head are durable metal
assemblies, the blade is an intentionally renewable consumable, cleaning is
local and simple, and the manufacturer publishes an exact model, material
description, blade-replacement instructions, and a long-running production
line. MERKUR describes the 34C as a closed-comb all-rounder with brass handle,
die-cast zinc head, and a stable Solingen identity. The model can remain in
service while only the standardized blade is renewed.

**Counter-case / open proof:** an all-stainless razor may have a stronger
corrosion/longevity case, an adjustable razor may serve a different Form, and
a cartridge or electric system may be safer for a user with different
constraints. The terminal packet needs an independent shaving-safety and
ergonomics source, corrosion/finish evidence, and a clear warning that blade
handling requires care.

**Sources:** [MERKUR 34C](https://www.merkur-razors.com/produkt/merkur-34c/?lang=en),
[MERKUR blade-replacement instructions](https://www.merkur-razors.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/safety-razor-two-piece-blade-replacement.pdf),
[MERKUR safety-razor range](https://www.merkur-razors.com/?lang=en).

### 99 — Hair Dryer

**Form:** A corded professional hair dryer for ordinary home or salon drying,
with a service path for the motor, switch, cord, filter/mesh, and thermal
components. Exclude cordless dryers, brush appliances, travel dual-voltage
models, and specialized salon systems whose electrical requirements differ.

**Proposed declaration:** Parlux **Alyon**, US-voltage variant, with the final
case recording color and exact electrical identifier.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** the Alyon has a corded 2,100-watt
professional motor, a stated 3,000-hour motor life, a ten-foot professional
cord, replaceable rear mesh/ring parts, and a manufacturer repair service that
uses authentic parts and charges a defined parts-and-labor fee. Its design is
not a sealed rechargeable appliance. The durability case is therefore based
on a serviceable motor-and-cord tool, not on a claim that the heating element
can never fail.

**Counter-case / open proof:** the Parlux 3800, 385 PowerLight, and newer
Digitalyon may win different Forms for energy, weight, or digital controls.
Before terminal issue, verify the exact US electrical specification, service
parts inventory, independent long-term motor evidence, and electrical/thermal
safety boundaries. Do not recommend use with an incompatible voltage system.

**Sources:** [Parlux Alyon US product page](https://parluxus.com/collections/all-dryers/products/parlux-alyon),
[Parlux repair service](https://parluxus.com/pages/repairservice),
[Alyon replacement ring and mesh](https://parluxus.com/products/replacement-part-parlux-ring-mesh),
[Parlux warranty](https://parluxus.com/pages/warranty).

### 100 — Umbrella

**Form:** A full-length, wind-resistant walking umbrella for one person,
designed for repeated urban use, with a modular canopy/frame/shaft/handle
architecture and a repair path. Exclude compact travel umbrellas, golf
umbrellas, parasols, and storm-specific commercial equipment.

**Proposed declaration:** BLUNT **Classic**, current full-length model.

**Why it survives the rescue pass:** BLUNT identifies four primary modular
components—canopy, frame, shaft, and handle—and publishes replacement and
repair tutorials for canopy, cap, tip, spring, and rewire work. The Classic's
canopy can be removed from the frame, the handle is threaded for replacement,
and the manufacturer describes a repair-before-replace program. Its standard
warranty is two years, with additional mechanical cover after registration;
out-of-warranty repairs remain available for a charge.

**Counter-case / open proof:** Davek's unconditional lifetime repair-or-
replacement guarantee is a serious counter-case, while a compact umbrella
serves a different carrying Form. The final packet needs independent wind
testing, long-term repair records, canopy replacement cost, and an exact
regional model record. A wind-resistant claim must not be treated as an
invitation to use any umbrella in unsafe storm conditions.

**Sources:** [BLUNT Classic](https://bluntumbrellas.com/products/blunt-classic),
[BLUNT repair tutorials](https://bluntumbrellas.com/pages/repair-tutorials),
[BLUNT warranty](https://bluntumbrellas.com/pages/warranty),
[Davek lifetime guarantee counter-case](https://eu.davekny.com/pages/lifetime-guarantee).

## What completing the Top 100 gives us

The backlog is now a researched editorial register rather than a flat list of
product names. Across the ten batches, the recurring structure is:

- a parent category that may be unresolved;
- one or more child Forms with explicit ordinary use and exclusions;
- a proposed exact model, where one exists;
- a permanence mechanism: repair, rebuild, warranty, or rational renewal;
- a counter-case that can overturn the proposed declaration; and
- evidence gaps that remain visible before adjudication.

That structure is the bridge to the mature site. The next implementation unit
should normalize the data as:

`Category → Form → Case → Ruling → Product → Evidence`

The public interface can then let a reader browse a broad category, see whether
it is resolved, and open the exact child Form and product dossier without
pretending that a single model represents every use case.
