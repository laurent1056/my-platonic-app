# Image permission request

> **Archived.** Platonic Ideal now uses interpretive plates only. This template
> is retained for historical context and is not part of the current public
> release workflow.

Use this note when requesting product photography from a manufacturer or
authorized seller. The request is deliberately specific: Platonic Ideal is an
editorial register, but it may later include paid dossiers and acquisition
links. Permission should cover the real intended use rather than relying on a
vague “press” approval.

## Request template

**Subject:** Request to use product photography in Platonic Ideal’s editorial register

Hello [name/team],

I publish Platonic Ideal, an independent editorial register that studies
durable products and names one model per category when it meets a published
standard. We are preparing the entry for **[product / exact model / variant]**.

We would like permission to display **[specific image filenames or URLs]** on
the product’s Platonic Ideal dossier at **[site URL]**, with a visible credit
and a link to **[your product page or brand site]**. The image would be used to
identify the exact model and its construction; it would not be altered to
suggest your endorsement of our verdict.

Please confirm whether you grant Platonic Ideal a non-exclusive, worldwide,
royalty-free license to reproduce and crop the supplied image(s) for:

- the Platonic Ideal website and responsive social previews;
- downloadable editorial dossiers or PDFs;
- newsletters and editorial announcements;
- future acquisition links or affiliate disclosures, if clearly labelled;
- archival copies of the published dossier.

We will retain your required credit, copyright notice, and attribution link.
We will not sell the image as a standalone asset, sublicense it, or use it in
paid advertising without asking separately. If the permission should expire or
is limited to a particular region, product variant, channel, or date, please
state those limits.

Thank you,

[name]  
Platonic Ideal  
[email]  
[site]

## Record the answer

Do not mark a source `licensed` from an informal “sure, that’s fine” unless
the person has authority to grant the permission. Save the message, approval
date, named image(s), permitted channels, territory, duration, required credit,
and any restrictions in the image manifest or its linked permission record.

The public image record should then include:

- `rightsStatus: licensed`;
- the permission record or archive reference;
- the exact model, variant, and region covered;
- the visible credit and source URL;
- the reviewer and approval date.

If permission is declined, expires, or cannot be verified, leave the source as
a purchase/identity lead and keep the local interpretive plate or glyph. That
is an editorial state, not a failure of the product or the ruling.
