# Platonic Ideal — Model-Faithful Plate Guide

This guide turns the image policy into a repeatable generation brief.

## The target

Create an antiquity-style interpretive plate that is close enough to the
declared model for the visitor to recognise its physical identity, but unlike
enough that it cannot be mistaken for the manufacturer's product image.

The working phrase is: **model-faithful, not model-identical**.

## Prompt recipe

Every prompt should include:

1. **The ruling:** category, declared model, and the Form quality being made
   visible.
2. **Three to five anchors:** silhouette, proportions, material, color,
   distinctive functional geometry, hardware, and relevant construction.
3. **The plate treatment:** weathered limestone or marble, charcoal basalt,
   restrained nimbus, carved relief or encaustic editorial illustration, square
   or 4:5 composition.
4. **The identity boundary:** no logos, wordmarks, readable text, packaging,
   serial/model marks, proprietary graphics, or copied source-photo framing.
5. **The disclosure:** the production record and rendered caption must identify
   the asset as an AI-generated interpretive plate, not product photography.

Use the following structure:

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: antiquity-style interpretive plate for the Platonic Ideal catalog
Declared category and model: [category] — [model]
Form quality to show: [the construction or permanence quality named by the ruling]
Visual anchors: [silhouette]; [material/finish/color]; [distinctive geometry];
  [relevant hardware or service feature]
Scene/backdrop: weathered limestone or marble against a charcoal basalt icon panel
Style/medium: antiquity-inspired editorial illustration, encaustic paint and
  carved relief, refined modern catalogue, not a photograph
Composition/framing: one centered subject, square or 4:5, generous margins,
  thin warm-gold nimbus
Lighting/mood: soft museum light, durable, quiet, useful
Text (verbatim): none
Constraints: model-informed but not model-identical; no brand identity;
  no copied source-photo composition
Avoid: logos, labels, readable text, packaging, serial/model marks, proprietary
  graphics, people, lifestyle scene, sale badge, watermark, exact product photo
```

## Review gate

Approve only when all of these are true:

- the category and declared model are recognisable from the object itself;
- the visual anchors support the written Form statement;
- the object is not an exact retail-photo recreation;
- no logo, label, readable text, packaging, or invented SKU claim appears;
- the plate remains visibly antiquity-inspired and catalogued;
- the caption and manifest disclose AI generation and representative status.

If recognisability is weak, add model-specific physical anchors. If identity
confusion is high, remove nonessential distinctive details, vary the source
composition, or regenerate.

## Example: cast-iron frying pan

For the Lodge 12-inch cast-iron skillet, useful anchors include a heavy single-
piece cast-iron body, deep curved sidewalls, a long handle, a helper handle,
pour spouts, and a matte seasoned-black surface. The image can preserve those
traits while changing the camera angle, relief setting, lighting, and exact
surface marks. It must not reproduce a Lodge logo, product stamp, packaging,
or a retailer's photograph.
