# Accessibility

## Implemented baseline

- semantic headings and page landmarks remain in order
- every control and route is keyboard reachable with a visible focus indicator
- primary controls and mobile register rows meet the 44px target
- verdicts use text and shape in addition to color
- light and dark text/state contrast meet WCAG 2.1 AA in the audited core views
- reduced-motion preferences disable transitions
- product glyphs and meaningful images provide accessible labels or alt text

## Practical checks

- tab through navigation and filters
- confirm visible focus states remain present
- verify text contrast on badges and cards
- ensure images have category-based alt text
- test at 200% zoom and with VoiceOver/NVDA before a formal accessibility claim
