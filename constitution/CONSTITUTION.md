# The Platonic Ideal Constitution

**Constitution ID:** `PI-CONSTITUTION`
**Version:** `1.0.0`
**Status:** Active
**Effective:** 2026-09-08

## Preamble

Platonic Ideal exists to make a judgment that ordinary recommendation systems
avoid: for a defined category, name one exact product that most completely
embodies the Form, or declare that no available product qualifies.

The authority of the register does not come from popularity, volume of content,
an opaque score, or commercial influence. It comes from applying the same public
rules to every case, preserving the evidence behind each ruling, exposing
uncertainty, and correcting the record without erasing it.

This Constitution is the normative editorial authority for every case, ruling,
challenge, dossier, and Oracle-assisted draft. If another project document or
interface conflicts with this text, this text governs for the Constitution
version cited by the case.

## Article I — Governing principles

### 1. One ideal per category

A category may have one current `DECLARED` model or one current `EMPTY` ruling.
There are no ties, runners-up, substitutes, preference matrices, or “best for
most people” lists in a terminal ruling. Historical rulings remain visible but
do not create multiple current ideals.

### 2. Finality over optimization

The purpose of a ruling is to end a reasonable search, not begin another round
of comparison. A declared object must reach the practical ceiling of the Form
without depending on luxury theater, novelty, or marginal specification gains.
Finality is conditional on the evidence remaining true; it is not a claim of
perfection or immortality.

### 3. Reversible failure

Expected failures must be repairable, rebuildable, maintainable, or rationally
renewable. A replaceable wear part is compatible with permanence. A disposable
critical core is not. Repair must remain practical in money, time, skill, parts,
and access relative to the category.

### 4. Model stability

A declaration names an exact, identifiable model or stable specification—not a
brand, family, or marketing label. Material changes in construction, parts,
service, software dependency, or model identity require review. Silent drift is
itself an integrity failure.

### 5. Emptiness is integrity

`EMPTY` is a positive finding, not missing content. If no examined product
passes every gate, the category remains empty indefinitely. “Best available” is
not an exception to the standard.

### 6. Revocation is public

No ruling is silently replaced. Amendments, confirmations, deprecations, and
revocations append to the public ruling ledger with their dates, reasons,
evidence receipts, and Constitution versions.

## Article II — Jurisdiction and the Form

Every case begins by defining the category before evaluating a product.

A valid category:

1. describes one coherent job to be done;
2. identifies the ordinary use context and material constraints;
3. is broad enough to be useful but narrow enough to support one Form;
4. states exclusions that would otherwise create incompatible Forms; and
5. does not use a preferred product's features to reverse-engineer the category.

If materially different users require incompatible objects, the category must
be redefined or marked `SPLIT_REQUIRED`. A regional law or operating condition
may be part of the category's scope when it changes what can safely or legally
perform the Form. Personal taste alone may not define jurisdiction.

The Form statement must describe essential function and constraints. It must
not contain a brand, model, winner, price point, trend, or aesthetic preference
unless that characteristic is itself functionally necessary.

## Article III — The admission test

A proposed declaration must pass all five gates. `UNKNOWN`, `PARTIAL`, or
`NOT_APPLICABLE` does not count as a pass. Failure of one gate makes a
`DECLARED` ruling inadmissible regardless of popularity or review scores.

### Gate G-01 — Stable Form

The category represents one coherent job and the proposed object directly
performs it without relying on an incompatible sub-form.

Required finding: the category definition, scope, and exclusions are explicit,
and the product is evaluated against that definition rather than against its
competitors' feature sets.

### Gate G-02 — Durable core

The object's useful core survives ordinary use on a time horizon appropriate to
the category. Materials, construction, and interfaces resist premature
replacement; cosmetic aging is not confused with functional failure.

Required finding: the case identifies the load-bearing materials and assemblies
and explains their expected behavior over time.

### Gate G-03 — Reversible failure

Expected failure modes can be prevented, diagnosed, repaired, rebuilt, or
rationally renewed. The remedy must be available in practice, not merely
theoretically possible.

Required finding: the case records likely failure modes, repair paths, parts,
skills, costs, and the point at which replacement becomes rational.

### Gate G-04 — Stable model

The exact product can be identified and repurchased or serviced without silent
specification drift. Its manufacturer, model identifier, production lineage,
critical parts, and material revisions are sufficiently legible to monitor.

Required finding: the case states how the model is identified and what changes
would trigger re-review.

### Gate G-05 — Ends the search

The product reaches the practical ceiling of the Form for the defined category.
Spending or comparing further yields mostly preference, status, novelty, or
marginal gains—not a materially more complete embodiment.

Required finding: the case states the strongest counter-case and explains why
it does not overturn the declaration. This is an editorial judgment supported
by evidence, never a numeric score.

## Article IV — Evidence standard

### 1. Minimum packet

A terminal ruling requires at least three admissible evidence items from at
least three independent evidence classes. At least one item must be primary
technical or service evidence, and at least one must be independent of the
manufacturer, seller, and any financially interested party.

The evidence classes are:

- `EV-01 MATERIALS_CONSTRUCTION` — specifications, construction, interfaces,
  manufacturing method, and critical materials;
- `EV-02 REPAIR_PATH` — manuals, teardown evidence, rebuild procedures, and
  diagnostic paths;
- `EV-03 PARTS_SERVICE` — parts catalogs, availability, service networks,
  tooling, and support commitments;
- `EV-04 FAILURE_LONGEVITY` — contextual longevity records, known failures,
  field use, and time behavior;
- `EV-05 MODEL_STABILITY` — production history, revision tracking, model
  identity, and evidence of drift or continuity;
- `EV-06 REPAIR_ECONOMICS` — parts, labor, shipping, downtime, and replacement
  thresholds;
- `EV-07 SAFETY_REGULATORY` — recalls, safety notices, certifications, and
  regulatory constraints;
- `EV-08 MARKET_COVERAGE` — the search protocol and credible alternatives
  examined, especially for an `EMPTY` finding.

Three pages repeating one press release are one source, not three. Evidence that
shares an origin, owner, test, or syndicated claim belongs to one independence
group and counts once toward independence.

### 2. What an evidence item records

Every evidence item has a permanent ID and records its source, publisher,
source type, evidence class, claim, relationship to the claim, retrieval date,
verification state, independence group, and any commercial relationship. A
source may support, contradict, or merely contextualize a claim.

### 3. Source roles

Manufacturer documentation is admissible for specifications, official parts,
and stated service procedures. It is not independent proof of longevity,
repair economics, or comparative superiority. Retail pages and marketing copy
may document availability or a claim, but cannot establish durability by
assertion.

Contextual professional use, repair records, long-term ownership reports, and
independent tests may contribute evidence when the use conditions and limits
are recorded. Testimonials, influencer endorsements, star ratings, popularity,
and one-off survival stories are not sufficient evidence of admission.

### 4. Facts, inference, and gaps

The case file and public evidence receipt distinguish:

- `DOCUMENTED_FACT` — directly supported by cited evidence;
- `EDITORIAL_INFERENCE` — a reasoned conclusion drawn from identified facts;
- `EVIDENCE_GAP` — information not established or not independently verified.

Inference may connect evidence to a ruling, but it may not masquerade as fact.
A material gap remains visible and prevents a terminal ruling when it affects an
admission gate, hard disqualifier, exact model identity, or confidence floor.

### 5. Contrary evidence

Contrary evidence is retained, not averaged away. The case must state its
strongest credible counter-case and disposition. When sources conflict, the
case explains why one is more probative or stays nonterminal.

## Article V — Hard disqualifiers

Any present hard disqualifier bars a `DECLARED` ruling. An `UNKNOWN` result on a
material disqualifier also bars declaration until resolved.

- `HD-01 SEALED_CRITICAL_SYSTEM` — a critical assembly is intentionally sealed
  or destructive to access, with no rational module-level service path.
- `HD-02 IRREVERSIBLE_WEAR_LAYER` — the primary function depends on a coating,
  bonded layer, or consumable surface that cannot be practically renewed.
- `HD-03 NONREPLACEABLE_CRITICAL_PART` — an expected-to-fail critical component
  has no compatible replacement, rebuild, or documented substitute.
- `HD-04 TERMINATING_PROPRIETARY_DEPENDENCY` — essential function or maintenance
  depends on proprietary software, authentication, consumables, or infrastructure
  whose foreseeable retirement ends use without a migration path.
- `HD-05 SILENT_MODEL_DRIFT` — the same model identity masks a material change
  in construction, components, serviceability, or performance.
- `HD-06 UNAVAILABLE_SERVICE_PATH` — repair requires unavailable parts, tools,
  documentation, authorization, or service access in the category's jurisdiction.
- `HD-07 IRRATIONAL_REPAIR_ECONOMICS` — ordinary critical failures predictably
  cost too much, take too long, or require too much access relative to rational
  replacement for the category.
- `HD-08 UNRESOLVED_TERMINAL_SAFETY_RISK` — credible safety or regulatory evidence
  makes ordinary ownership unacceptable and no verified remedy exists.
- `HD-09 CATEGORY_MISMATCH` — the product wins only by changing, narrowing, or
  evading the defined Form after evaluation began.
- `HD-10 EVIDENCE_INTEGRITY_FAILURE` — material claims rely on fabricated,
  unverifiable, undisclosed commercially interested, or materially misleading
  evidence.

Scarcity, high maintenance, slow service, old technology, niche use, or fuel
consumption may be important flags without being automatic bans. The case must
explain their effect rather than smuggle them in as preference.

## Article VI — States and verdicts

The only current terminal verdicts are:

- `DECLARED` — one exact model passes every gate, no hard disqualifier is
  present or materially unknown, and the evidence and confidence floors hold;
- `EMPTY` — the defined category has been seriously searched and no examined
  product can honestly be declared under this Constitution.

The nonterminal working states are:

- `DRAFT` — jurisdiction or initial research is incomplete;
- `CANDIDATE` — a promising subject exists but evidence or a gate is incomplete;
- `SPLIT_REQUIRED` — the category contains incompatible Forms;
- `CONDITIONAL` — the proposed outcome depends on an unresolved operating
  condition or jurisdiction;
- `CONSUMABLE` — rational renewal may be intrinsic to the Form and requires a
  specific permanence argument;
- `UNDER_REVIEW` — a former or proposed ruling is being re-adjudicated.

These labels describe work; they are not softer recommendations. They must not
be presented as declarations.

`DEPRECATED` describes a historical ruling displaced by an appended amendment,
supersession, or revocation event. Deprecated records remain public.

## Article VII — Terminal-ruling admissibility

### 1. Requirements for `DECLARED`

Before issue, the case file must contain:

1. a stable category and Form definition;
2. one exact manufacturer and model identifier;
3. `PASS` on all five admission gates;
4. `ABSENT` on all ten hard disqualifiers;
5. the minimum independent evidence packet;
6. documented maintenance, likely failure modes, and repair economics;
7. a strongest counter-case and reasoned disposition;
8. a confidence score of at least `4/5`;
9. an adjudicator, decision date, Constitution version, and evidence receipt;
10. a complete public ruling ready to remain free.

### 2. Requirements for `EMPTY`

Before issue, the case file must contain:

1. a stable category and Form definition;
2. a recorded market-search protocol and credible candidate set;
3. the material gate failures or disqualifying patterns for each serious
   candidate or for the category's shared architecture;
4. the minimum independent evidence packet, including market coverage;
5. an explicit statement of what a future qualifying product would require;
6. a strongest counter-case to emptiness and reasoned disposition;
7. a confidence score of at least `4/5`;
8. an adjudicator, decision date, Constitution version, and evidence receipt;
9. a complete public ruling ready to remain free.

### 3. Confidence

Confidence describes the completeness and resilience of the case, not product
quality and not the editor's enthusiasm:

- `0` — unassessed;
- `1` — anecdotal lead;
- `2` — initial evidence with material unknowns;
- `3` — plausible case, not publication-grade;
- `4` — publication-grade evidence, counter-case addressed;
- `5` — repeatedly reverified or independently counter-reviewed.

No terminal ruling may be issued below `4`. A `5` is not required and cannot be
claimed merely because many low-quality sources agree.

## Article VIII — Publication and the evidence receipt

Every terminal ruling receives a permanent ruling ID and a versioned,
append-only ledger event. The free public ruling includes:

- the verdict and exact model, if declared;
- the category's Form, scope, and exclusions;
- the core reasoning and why the search ends or stays open;
- gate and disqualifier summaries;
- failure modes, maintenance, and repair economics;
- confidence, last verification date, and Constitution version;
- a public evidence receipt separating facts, inference, and gaps;
- commercial disclosures; and
- links to the ruling history and challenge record.

The evidence receipt lists evidence IDs, classes, verification dates, and claim
roles. It is proof of process, not a decorative source count.

The paid dossier may add research convenience and depth: source-level notes,
extended candidate analysis, maintenance and sourcing detail, counterarguments,
and monitoring history. It may not withhold the verdict or the reasoning needed
to evaluate the free ruling.

## Article IX — Challenges

A public challenge is admissible only when it contests an existing ruling by:

1. naming a specific admission gate, hard disqualifier, material claim,
   evidence-integrity issue, model-drift event, or revocation trigger;
2. stating a falsifiable objection; and
3. supplying verifiable evidence or identifying a material contradiction in
   the published record.

Brand preference, popularity, a competing product nomination, disagreement
without evidence, or payment is not an admissible challenge. The docket is not
a voting system.

Admissibility decisions and final dispositions are public. An admitted
challenge may be `UPHELD`, `DISMISSED`, produce an `AMENDED` ruling, or produce
a `REVOKED` ruling. Private contact information and abuse-prevention data never
enter the public docket.

## Article X — Review, amendment, and revocation

A ruling is reviewed when credible evidence indicates:

- material construction or specification change;
- loss of parts, service, repair documentation, or practical repair access;
- a new or newly understood irreversible failure mode;
- repair economics no longer remain rational;
- an unresolved safety, recall, legal, or regulatory problem;
- evidence was materially wrong, incomplete, fabricated, or misinterpreted;
- the model is discontinued and the declaration's availability premise no
  longer holds; or
- a challenger demonstrates that the original ruling failed its own Form or
  admission test.

A new entrant does not displace a declaration merely by being newer, more
expensive, more popular, or marginally better. Replacement requires a new case
showing that the incumbent no longer qualifies or that the prior adjudication
was materially wrong.

Every change appends a ledger event. Prior text, evidence receipts, and verdicts
remain addressable. A revoked declaration resolves to `EMPTY` or to a new
separately adjudicated declaration; it never disappears.

## Article XI — Commercial independence

Editorial outcome and commercial outcome are separate systems.

- No maker, seller, sponsor, affiliate, or purchaser may buy a declaration,
  priority, placement, score, challenge disposition, or guaranteed review.
- The Constitution and complete public ruling remain free.
- Payment may purchase the deeper evidence dossier and research convenience,
  never a different verdict or hidden decisive reasoning.
- Financial relationships, supplied products, affiliate links, and material
  conflicts are disclosed in the case and public ruling.
- Affiliate revenue, if ever introduced, may follow a ruling but may not select,
  order, or preserve one.
- Paid petitions and sponsored adjudications require a future constitutional
  amendment and explicit product decision; they are not authorized by v1.0.0.

Hosted checkout and private fulfillment are operational conveniences, not
editorial inputs.

## Article XII — The Oracle

The Oracle is a private authoring instrument. It may structure a draft, identify
missing fields, propose counterarguments, or critique a case against this
Constitution. It may not:

- issue, sign, publish, amend, or revoke a ruling;
- create evidence by assertion;
- treat a generated citation as verified;
- silently fill an evidence gap;
- write directly to the canonical ledgers; or
- override a human adjudicator.

Oracle output remains noncanonical until a human verifies every material claim,
adds admissible evidence, runs the institutional validator, and commits the
case through the ordinary editorial process.

## Article XIII — Versioning and amendment

Each case and ruling pins the Constitution version used for adjudication.
Later amendments do not rewrite historical cases.

Constitution versions use semantic versioning:

- **major** — changes a governing principle, terminal verdict, admission gate,
  disqualifier, evidence floor, commercial boundary, or amendment authority;
- **minor** — adds a compatible evidence class, process requirement, or
  clarification that can alter future admissibility without reversing a core
  principle;
- **patch** — corrects language or implementation metadata without changing
  admissibility.

Every amendment records the proposer, rationale, effective date, changed
sections, and migration impact. A major or minor release must state which active
rulings require re-review. No amendment is retroactive without an explicit,
public re-adjudication event.

The machine-enforceable profile is released beside this text and pinned to this
file's SHA-256 digest. A digest mismatch is a release-blocking error.

## Article XIV — Limits of the method

Platonic Ideal is a published standard and editorial judgment, not personalized
advice, a guarantee, or a claim that one object fits every body, jurisdiction,
budget, risk tolerance, or specialized use.

Evidence can be incomplete, products can change, and the institution can be
wrong. The remedy is explicit uncertainty, admissible challenge, public
revision, and preserved history—not concealed subjectivity or false precision.

The register ends a search only within the category and Form it actually
defines.

## Appendix B — Terminal checklist

A release validator must reject a terminal ruling when any answer is “no”:

- Is the case pinned to an active Constitution version?
- Is the category definition stable and non-circular?
- Is the verdict exactly `DECLARED` or `EMPTY`?
- Does a declaration name exactly one identifiable model?
- Are all five admission findings complete?
- Are all ten hard-disqualifier findings complete?
- Does the evidence packet satisfy count, class, independence, and source-role
  requirements?
- Do all claims and findings reference existing evidence IDs?
- Are material gaps absent from terminal criteria?
- Are failure modes, maintenance, and repair economics explicit?
- Is the strongest counter-case addressed?
- Is confidence at least `4/5`?
- Are commercial relationships disclosed?
- Is the free public ruling complete?
- Will issue append, rather than overwrite, the public ledger?
