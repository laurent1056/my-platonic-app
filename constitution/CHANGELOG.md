# Constitution changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-08

- Made `EMPTY` a last-resort terminal finding with an explicit
  declaration-rescue pass before issue.
- Clarified that a declaration seeks the best complete embodiment of the
  essential Form, not perfection: ordinary trade-offs, replaceable wear parts,
  maintenance, and rational renewal may be compatible with `DECLARED`.
- Required a stable, non-split Form before an `EMPTY` ruling and clarified that
  unresolved category conflicts remain nonterminal.
- Clarified that a hard disqualifier depends on the Form's appropriate
  maintenance or renewal path, not on the absence of replacement for every
  component.
- Reclassified the founding pocket-knife research case as `SPLIT_REQUIRED`;
  there are no terminal rulings requiring re-review in the active ledger.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-08

- Established six governing principles.
- Defined five binary admission gates and ten hard disqualifiers.
- Required three admissible items from three independent evidence classes,
  including primary technical/service evidence and one financially independent
  source.
- Defined `DECLARED` and `EMPTY` as the only current terminal verdicts.
- Set the terminal confidence floor at `4/5`.
- Established public evidence receipts, an append-only ruling ledger, a formal
  challenge docket, public revocation, and commercial independence.
- Restricted the Oracle to noncanonical drafting and critique.
