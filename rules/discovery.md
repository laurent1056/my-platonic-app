# Discovery rules

> How this PM runs continuous discovery. Loaded when the system runs interviews, surveys, or opportunity framing.

## Cadence
<!-- Target frequency of customer touchpoints. Common pattern: weekly interviews (Teresa Torres rhythm). -->
No external discovery cadence yet. The current loop is founder-led and
on-demand: Laurent uses the product, notices friction, and records the next
question. A weekly Friday `/review` is a maintenance ritual, not a customer
interview cadence.

## Where transcripts live
<!-- Source-of-truth for raw interview transcripts. -->
No external interview corpus exists. Future transcripts belong in
`source/interviews/` with synthesized records in `ingestion/interviews/`.

## Opportunity-solution tree
<!-- If using OST: where the tree lives, how it's updated, how opportunities link to hypotheses. -->
No opportunity-solution tree is currently maintained. Keep the product's
category register and strategy tensions as the current framing layer.

## Recruitment rules
<!-- Who counts as a valid participant. Avoid biased samples. -->
Not applicable yet. When external validation begins, recruit people who have
recently made durable-goods purchase decisions in categories represented by the
register; do not use only friends or people already persuaded by the thesis.

## Question quality
<!-- Behavioral over hypothetical. Specific past instances over general patterns. -->
Ask about a recent category search, what alternatives were considered, what
evidence changed the decision, and whether the singular verdict was useful. Do
not treat “would you use this?” as behavioral evidence.
